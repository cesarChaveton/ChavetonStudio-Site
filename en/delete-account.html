DominionHex — Delete your account and data

Developer: Chaveton Studios

Two ways to delete, depending on whether you have the app installed

DominionHex saves your progress with an anonymous account or, if you linked it, with your Google account. You can delete the account and all of its cloud and device data at any time.

From the game

If you have the app installed: open Settings → Account → "Delete my account and data" and confirm with "Delete account". The cloud profile, progress and balance are deleted, along with the account. On this phone too. If the account is linked to Google, to confirm it is you the game asks you to choose the same Google account. If you choose another, cancel, or there is no connection, nothing is deleted. If the account is anonymous, it is deleted with a connection. Nothing is deleted without a connection.

Without the game installed (Google-linked account)

If you linked your account to Google and no longer have the game installed, write to us at privacidad from that same Google account's email, with the subject "Delete DominionHex account". We complete the deletion within a maximum of 30 days and confirm it to you by email.

Accounts without Google linked (anonymous)

If you never linked a Google account, your account is anonymous: we cannot identify it or contact you from outside the game, so deletion must be done from the app (see above). If an anonymous account is inactive for 12 months, we delete it automatically.

What gets deleted

  • Your access account: its identifier and, if you had linked it, the email and name of your Google account.
  • Your profile name, avatar and piece.
  • Your progress: cosmetic unlocks, stages cleared, tutorial, missions and map rewards.
  • Your gem balance and the transactions that changed it.

What is kept

Nothing tied to you as a player. The provider (Google) may keep technical backup copies for up to 180 days before permanently removing them from its systems.

More information

You can read the full detail in the DominionHex privacy policy.
