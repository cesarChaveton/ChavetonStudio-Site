Support — Magician's Ally

Need help?

Write to us at with your phone model, your Android version and a description of what happened. We reply by email within a few business days.

Frequently asked questions

How does the free plan work? Each day you get two performance sessions, each with up to ten reveals. If you run out, you can watch an optional ad to get one more session.

Does the app need internet? Not to perform. You only need a connection to buy, restore purchases or watch a rewarded ad.

I bought something and it doesn't show up. Open the app with an internet connection and use the restore purchases option in settings. If it still doesn't appear, write to us with your Google Play account email and the purchase date.

Where are my photos stored? Only on your phone. We don't receive them. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

Can I ask for a refund? Refunds for purchases made on Google Play are handled through Google Play, under its policies.

Can you explain how to do a trick? Inside the app you'll find how each tool works. Out of respect for the art, we don't explain methods by email or in public.
