The story

Your phone has been on the table all night. Nobody has touched it.

Someone thinks of a card. They don't say it. Maybe they draw it in the air, maybe they just imagine it.

You pick up the phone. On the screen, a face-down card waits, with a barely visible glow along its edge. You ask your spectator to touch it.

The card turns over. It's theirs.

What happened in between is your business. Magician's Ally only makes sure nobody finds out.

What it is

Magician's Ally is a collection of digital tools for magicians who perform live card magic. It combines your real deck with your phone to create appearances, transformations and cards that move in and out of the screen.
