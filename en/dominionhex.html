At a glance

What it is, where it runs, and how to get in

1

What it is

A turn-based strategy game. You move your pieces across a board of hexagons and face AI-controlled rivals. Each piece is a character.

2

Where it runs

On Android phones and tablets, with a touch screen. We are starting with Android and iOS is coming soon. Your progress is saved to an anonymous account, with no sign-up needed, and you can play offline.

3

How to get in

It is free. When it launches, you will download it from Google Play on your Android phone or tablet; iOS will follow.

Questions

What people ask us most

Can I play from this page?

No. This page only presents the game. DominionHex is an app you install on an Android phone or tablet.

What about iPhone?

Coming soon to iOS. We are starting with Android, on phones and tablets, and the plan is to reach all platforms. We do not have a date for computers yet.

Do I need internet to play?

You can play offline: matches and your changes are saved on your device and synced to your account when the connection returns. You need internet to download the app and to save your progress to the cloud.

Does it have ads?

No. DominionHex has no ads and we want to keep it that way for as long as we can.

Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free. Some characters and pieces will be purchasable with real money soon.

When is it coming to the stores?

There is no date yet. We will announce it here when it is available.
